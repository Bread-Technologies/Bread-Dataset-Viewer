# Philosophical Lineage

Reference document for the eventual paper. The Lean files are pure
mathematics; this document records the philosophical commitments that
motivate the formal targets.

## Persistence: stage theory and process philosophy

The framework's Heraclitean treatment of persistence draws on two
distinct traditions:

**Analytic stage theory.**

- **W. V. O. Quine** — *Word and Object* (1960); *Ontological Relativity*
  (1968). The "gavagai" argument: reference is inscrutable; "rabbit"
  might equally well denote whole rabbits, rabbit-stages, or
  undetached rabbit parts with no fact of the matter selecting one.
  Ontological relativity: what exists depends on the framework
  adopted. **Two roles in this project:**
  - Defends the Rortian "real is honorific" commitment with analytic
    rigor — reference being inscrutable means "real" does pragmatic
    work, not metaphysical natural-kind work.
  - Prefigures at the semantic level what Rovelli's relational QM
    does at the physical level: what exists depends on the framework.
    The same move, made twice, half a century apart, in different
    domains. The paper should draw this parallel explicitly.

- **David Lewis** — *On the Plurality of Worlds*. Four-dimensionalism;
  ordinary objects as temporally extended entities with temporal
  parts at each moment. The seminal analytic statement of perdurance.

- **Theodore Sider** — *Four-Dimensionalism: An Ontology of Persistence
  and Time* (2001). Canonical contemporary defense of stage theory.
  Cite Sider for any technical claim about persistence-via-stages.

- **Derek Parfit** — *Reasons and Persons*. Person-stages inherit
  directly from this tradition; the framework completes (rather than
  rejects) Parfit's project that personal identity isn't what matters.

**Process philosophy.**

- **A. N. Whitehead** — *Process and Reality*; *Modes of Thought*.
  Actual occasions of experience; subjective aim; panexperientialism.
  Metaphysically richer cousin of stage theory.
- **Henri Bergson** — *durée* over spatialized time; motion as
  fundamental; response to Zeno.

**Eastern.**

- **Madhyamaka (Nāgārjuna)** and **Yogacara** on *anatta*; the
  Pudgalavada controversy on provisional self.
- *Paticca-samuppada* (conditioned arising) — persistence of wanting
  across moments without persistent substance; structurally analogous
  to flames passed candle to candle.

## Audience-dependent presentation

- **Continental / process readers**: lead with Whitehead.
- **Analytic readers**: lead with Lewis and Sider. Their formal
  apparatus makes the Heraclitean view respectable in mainstream
  metaphysics rather than continental hand-waving.
- **Buddhist-trained readers**: lead with Madhyamaka.

Omitting Lewis/Sider in analytic-facing exposition signals naivety
about the contemporary literature; do not do this.

## Unified extension claim

The framework's persistence claims should be framed as a **unified
extension of stage theory across all ontological categories** —
selves, ships, ordinary objects, fundamental particles — rather than
as a sui generis metaphysical claim. The mathematical structure
(Wantable + Meeting + Pattern stability under transformation) is
agnostic between the various lineages and applies uniformly.

## Quine and Rovelli in parallel

  Quine (semantic):  what `rabbit` refers to depends on translation manual
  Rovelli (physical): what facts hold depends on which observer

Both deny an observer-/framework-independent ledger; both retain
pragmatic adequacy of the framework-internal claims. The framework
unifies these by making *perspective with directedness* the
fundamental thing and showing that the semantic and physical layers
are different surfaces of the same underlying structure.

## Other lineage (from main prompt, summarized)

- **Idealist core**: Berkeley, Schopenhauer (Will-as-noumenon —
  closest Western analog), Husserl, Sartre.
- **Kantian**: Kant (transcendental aesthetic / deduction template),
  Cassirer, Friedman (relativized a priori).
- **Relational/structural**: Leibniz, Mach, Rovelli, Wheeler.
- **Pragmatist**: James, Peirce, Rorty.
- **Existential/relational ethics**: Buber, Levinas, Heidegger.
- **Economic/Austrian**: Hayek (spontaneous order, distributed
  knowledge), Mises, Smith, Kirzner.
- **Information-theoretic**: Jaynes, Friston.
- **Continental wild card**: Deleuze & Guattari (use sparingly).

## Positions confronted and rejected

- **Dennett** — compatibilism. The framework rejects physicalism, so
  needs no compatibilist rescue.
- **Goff** — *Galileo's Error*; panpsychism as colorful physicalism.
  Framework transcends: matter doesn't HAVE consciousness; matter IS
  how consciousness appears under perspectivization.
- **Hume** — bundle theory. Partially right but misses the structural
  persistence stage theory captures.
- **Parfit** — completed rather than rejected.
