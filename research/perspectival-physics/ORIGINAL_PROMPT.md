## 1. The Axioms

Two axioms. Everything else is theorem, definition, or contingent
universe-specific structure.

**Axiom I (Existence).** To be is to want. Wanting is intrinsically
directional (it points from lack toward fulfillment) and intrinsically
relational (it presupposes a complement).

**Axiom II (Actualization).** Wants meet their complements through
actualization events. Actualization is irreversible: what has been
actualized cannot return to potential. Reality is the developing
structure of potential and actualized meetings.

The old Axiom V (asymmetry / irreversibility / arrow of time) is
folded into Axiom II. NOT a separate axiom — part of what
actualization IS.

Definitions following the axioms:

- *Perspective:* position in the relational web of wantings. Not
  substance, not primitive. The structural position from which
  directedness obtains.
- *Meeting:* event where complementary wants clear. Two modalities —
  *potential* (not yet cleared) and *actualized* (cleared, settled,
  irreversibly part of the structure).
- *Particular:* stable pattern of wantings and meetings, recognizable
  across time. Selves, things, fields, laws are all particulars in
  this sense. None are substances.
- *Reality:* developing structure of meetings (potential and
  actualized) as it unfolds.

## 2. The Two-Tier Architecture (CENTRAL TO v2)

**Tier A: Irreversible meeting-structure.**

The foundational level. Wants seek complements. Some meetings
actualize (clearings happen). Once actualized, cannot become
potential again. Asymmetric flow from potential to actual = the arrow
of time, constitutive rather than emergent. Mathematically:
many-to-one maps (multiple potential meetings collapsing into one
definite outcome) require nonconservative dynamics. Forced by
Picard-Lindelöf applied to time-reversal-symmetric ODEs — see
Witkowski-Brown-Truong 2024.

**Tier B: Reversible limit.**

What you get by bracketing actualization events and describing only
the inter-event structure of evolving potential. Wants in play,
complements available, relational evolution — describable reversibly
because by construction nothing irreversible occurs within the
bracketed intervals. Mathematical structure that emerges, constrained
by operational consistency with the bracketing events, takes the form
of Hilbert space with unitary evolution (QM) and Lorentzian manifolds
with field equations (GR).

The relationship Tier A → Tier B is the *limit operation*: bracket
actualizations, describe what remains. The relationship Tier B →
standard physics is the operational reconstruction line (Hardy, CBH,
Müller). Prior R6/R7 work lives entirely at Tier B.

**The seams.** Tier A leaks through Tier B at specific places:

- Measurement / collapse in QM (irreversible actualization seen from
  within the reversible description).
- Vertex preservation (constraint that pure states preserve under
  reversibility — Tier A's discreteness commitment visible at
  Tier B).
- Erasure events (Witkowski et al. 2024 Lyapunov instability at the
  reset point).
- Decoherence (Tier A actualization-flow visible as effective
  irreversibility in Tier B).

The framework's distinctive empirical content lives at these seams.
